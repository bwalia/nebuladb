Leaving mock-384: real embeddings without tribal wipe lore

For a long time the NebulaDB showcase health chip showed model: mock-384. Chat already used a real Ollama gateway. Vectors did not. Semantic search was a deterministic hash; hybrid and exact lookup still worked, but “find something like this” meant nothing.

Why we stayed on mock

HNSW is single-dimension. Snapshots stamp that dimension. Changing NEBULA_EMBED_DIM from 384 to 768 refuses boot. Export/import copies vectors, so it cannot re-embed. Folklore said wipe the PVC and reload Companies House from CSV — multi-day, multi-region, and the same trap for the next model.

What we shipped

POST /api/v1/admin/embed/migrate dumps live document texts to $NEBULA_DATA_DIR/embed-migrate/docs.jsonl, writes pending.json, then waits. Restart with the public gateway embedder (nomic-embed-text @ 768, x-api-key auth shared with chat). Boot archives the old snapshot/WAL when pending matches, opens empty, and drains the jsonl with a durable cursor.

Followers and region-b take a fresh .nsnap via scripts/bootstrap_region_mirror.sh after the leader is done.
